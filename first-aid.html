Common mistakes

Slow down before using a familiar home remedy or moving an injured person.

THE MISTAKE

Delaying emergency help

Why it matters: A serious or life-threatening condition needs professional assessment and response.

Do instead: Check scene safety, call appropriate emergency services, and follow dispatcher instructions.

Source: American Red Cross First Aid Steps

THE MISTAKE

Moving someone unnecessarily

Why it matters: Movement can create additional risk when a head, neck, or spinal injury is possible.

Do instead: Keep the person still unless there is immediate danger, and get qualified help.

Source: American Red Cross First Aid Steps

THE MISTAKE

Giving food or drink to an unconscious person

Why it matters: A person who is not fully awake may not be able to safely swallow.

Do instead: Check responsiveness and breathing, call for help, and follow dispatcher instructions.

Source: American Red Cross First Aid Steps
