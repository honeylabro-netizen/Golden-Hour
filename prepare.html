Planning screen / module 03

Prepare

Turn general preparedness ideas into a plan for home, school, family, travel, and the needs of people and pets.

LOCALIZE YOUR PLANConfirm routes, contacts, assembly areas, and official instructions for your own location.
HOME

Emergency kit

Consider supplies for water, food, lighting, communication, first aid, medication, hygiene, documents, and household-specific needs.

SCHOOL

School readiness

Know evacuation routes, assembly areas, official communication channels, drill expectations, and accessibility arrangements.

FAMILY

Communication plan

Choose contacts, a meeting point, an out-of-area contact if appropriate, and a way to account for household members.

TRAVEL

Travel and pets

Consider destination hazards, transport contacts, medication, mobility needs, and safe arrangements for pets.

BACKUP

Documents and power

Keep important information accessible and plan for safe lighting, charging, and communication when power is unavailable.

EVACUATION

Routes and meeting point

Learn official routes and identify a safe meeting place. Follow local authority instructions during an event.
