LEARNING STATION / 01

FIRST 60 SECONDS

The first minute can be critical. Know your first actions. This is a learning visualization, not a countdown.

60SECONDS
  1. 01Stay as calm as possible.
  2. 02Check whether the area is safe.
  3. 03Assess the immediate danger.
  4. 04Call appropriate emergency services.
  5. 05Give basic help only when safe.
  6. 06Follow dispatcher instructions.

LEARNING STATION / 02

CPR / SAVE A LIFE

When cardiac arrest happens, fast action matters. Learning the basics can help a bystander act instead of freezing or waiting.

CALL↓PUSH↓HELP
  1. STEP 01CALL FOR EMERGENCY HELP

    Call 911 in the Philippines, or have another person call.

  2. STEP 02CHECK RESPONSE AND NORMAL BREATHING

    If someone is unresponsive and not breathing normally, treat it as a medical emergency.

  3. STEP 03START HANDS-ONLY CPR

    For a suddenly collapsed teen or adult, push hard and fast in the center of the chest at 100–120 compressions per minute.

  4. STEP 04USE AN AED

    Turn it on and follow its spoken or visual prompts, alongside dispatcher instructions.

  5. STEP 05KEEP GOING

    Continue until professional responders take over or the person shows signs of recovery.

WHY CPR MATTERS

Cardiac arrest can make a person suddenly unresponsive and stop breathing normally. CPR helps maintain blood flow while further emergency treatment is arranged.

LEARNING NOTE: This is a quick educational reminder, not a substitute for hands-on CPR/AED training. In a real emergency, call emergency services and follow dispatcher instructions.

Source: American Heart Association CPR guidance. Last reviewed: September 18, 2026.
