Safety literacy

Common emergency mistakes

Reliable response starts with avoiding extra exposure and unverified instructions.

THE MISTAKE

Going back into danger

Why it matters: Re-entry can expose people to fire, smoke, structural damage, or other hazards.

Do instead: Stay in a safe location and wait for authorities to give an all-clear.

Source: American Red Cross fire safety

THE MISTAKE

Relying on rumors

Why it matters: Incorrect warnings can send people toward hazards or delay evacuation.

Do instead: Use current official warnings and local emergency instructions.

Source: DOST-PAGASA official information

THE MISTAKE

Investigating a suspected gas leak

Why it matters: A suspected leak can create an unsafe environment.

Do instead: Leave if safe, avoid creating ignition sources, and contact appropriate responders.

Source: Follow local fire and public-safety instructions.
